**Status:** template only. Do not collect data until the coordinator has

## Study

TurboJET Discovery beta paired evaluation. You will provide two comparable
requirements requests. One output comes from the candidate Discovery flow and
one from a baseline prompt. You are not asked to promote TurboJET.

## What is recorded

- Your request text and both outputs.
- Time, model/tool calls, token usage and cost where reported (`unknown`

artifacts. Outputs are labelled only A/B for blinded review. A coordinator
holds the arm key until scores are submitted.
